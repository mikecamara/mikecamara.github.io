Daiet

Daiet helps users generate personalized diet plans and body-composition progress insights. AI processing is powered by Google Gemini and cloud history is stored in Firebase.

App StoreGoogle PlayPrivacy Policy

How data use works

  • Users enter meal-planning prompts and can upload body photos for progress analysis.
  • Before any AI request is sent, users must explicitly accept the in-app AI data sharing consent prompt.
  • Prompt text and images are processed by Google Gemini, with plan/diary history saved to Firebase for app functionality.
  • Users can review or withdraw consent and can request full cloud data deletion from the app settings/account screens.
